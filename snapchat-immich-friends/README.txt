SNAPCHAT MEMORIES -> IMMICH / GOOGLE PHOTOS / ICLOUD / ANY CLOUD FOLDER
(Windows and Mac)

1. Install Python 3.9+ from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   (Windows: tick "Add python.exe to PATH".)
2. Windows: double-click START-WINDOWS.bat
   Mac:     double-click START-MAC.command
            (If macOS blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway,
             or run "bash START-MAC.command" in Terminal.)
3. In the window:
   - "Send to": choose Immich, Google Photos, Apple iCloud Photos, or any folder
     (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, NAS...). Click "Test connection".
   - Paste your Snapchat download links (one per line) or click "Add ZIPs...".
   - Click "Start import". You can stop and resume later.

Google Photos needs a one-time free setup (your own OAuth "Desktop app" client).
Step-by-step instructions are in README.md.

Downloaded files are kept in the "Snapchat-Immich-Import" folder in your home folder
until you delete them. Never share your Snapchat links, Immich API key or Google
client file with anyone.
