SNAPCHAT MEMORIES MOVER  (Windows and Mac)

EASIEST: download the ready-made app (no Python needed):
  https://github.com/MMCQ-lab/snapchat-to-immich#download

RUNNING FROM THIS FOLDER INSTEAD:
1. Install Python 3.9+ from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   (Windows: tick "Add python.exe to PATH".)
2. Windows: double-click START-WINDOWS.bat
   Mac:     double-click START-MAC.command
            (If macOS blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.)

IN THE APP:
  1. Choose where your memories go: Google Photos, iCloud Photos, a folder, or Immich.
  2. Paste your Snapchat download links, or add the ZIP files.
  3. Click "Start moving my memories". It checks you have enough space first.
  Every step has a Help button. You can pause and continue later.

Nothing is deleted from Snapchat or your computer. Downloads are kept in the
"Snapchat-Immich-Import" folder in your home folder until you delete them.
Never share your Snapchat links or keys with anyone.
