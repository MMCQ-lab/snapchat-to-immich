## Download

| Your computer | Download this |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | **SnapchatMemories-Windows.exe** |
| **Mac with Apple chip** (M1, M2, M3, M4… most Macs from 2021 on) | **SnapchatMemories-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip** |
| **Older Mac with Intel chip** | **SnapchatMemories-Mac-Intel.zip** |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple…" means Apple chip; "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.

## Opening it the first time

**Windows:** double-click the file. If a blue box says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Mac:** double-click the ZIP, then drag **Snapchat Memories** into your **Applications** folder and open it. If it says it *"can't be opened"*, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**.

These warnings appear because the app is shared privately between friends rather than sold through Microsoft or Apple. You only see them once.

Then follow the three steps in the app. The **Help** buttons explain each one.
